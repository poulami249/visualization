Link To Dataset - https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/outbreaks-in-toronto-healthcare-institutions/

> What software did you use to create your data visualization?
I used Microsoft Power BI Desktop version to create the visualization.

> Who is your intended audience? 
My intended audience is public health authorities in City of Toronto who are looking for statistics related to outbreaks in the city and are proactively looking to reduce the incidence of infectious outbreaks in the city.

> What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization? 
I am trying to convey how the average duration of each type of outbreaks differ from each other with respect to the type of infection and outbreak setting.

> What design principles (substantive, perceptual, aesthetic) did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply these principles? With what elements of your plots? 
I included the following principles in my visualization:
1. Substantive - I am trying to emphasize on the clarity of information as it is conveyed by the data by keeping it easy to understand with simple charts like the clustered column chart which is showing the average duration of outbreaks by each infection category by each outbreak setting.
2. Perceptual - I added three different colors to differentiate between the different infection and also added legend to improve perception.
3. Aesthetics - I ensured these are aesthetic with large enough labels/titles/legends. The colors are used to ensure that people belonging to all vision capabilities - normal/red-green colorblind and blue-yellow colorblind  people can decipher. There is also a Alt-text that's added to describe what the chart is trying to convey.

> How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible?  
To make it accessible and easy to understand I have done the following:
1. Added simple clustered column charts, labels, and legends
2. Added accessible color palette
3. Added Alt-text to keep the cognitive load low

> Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization?  
The public health authorities and the healthcare institutions that are talked about in the dataset would benefit from this data as it would provide insights on the type of infection and the type of setting that are contributing to most of the longer outbreak incidents. This would eventually help them strategize and take necessary precautionary steps to proactively lower the incidence of certain infectious diseases. For example, in this data visualization we can see the Hospital Acute Care experiences the longest average outbreak incidents from Other type of infections and hence they might want to strengthen their hygeine and sanitation rules, food safety regulations and impose strict infection control procedures.

> How did you choose which features of your chosen dataset to include or exclude from your visualization?
I have chosen features based on what I wanted the visualization to convey. I wanted to highlight the average lifespan of these outbreaks based on each outbreak settings and infection type and disover if a particular setting/infection is contributing to longer ourbreak incidents. Hence, I created a column called Average Duration of Outbreak (Days) by calculating the difference between end and start date. I also used the two features - type of outbreak and outbreak setting.
